# Customer action pane: D365 research and implementation

References: the six supplied images (Invoice, Collect, two identical Service images, Customer, and Customer balance). Toolbar labels are reference material, not instructions to enable advanced modules. Research uses Microsoft Learn, not Dynamics 365 Sales CRM or Business Central.

## Tabs

| Tab               | D365 purpose and basic-scope decision                                                    | Current frontend                                                                                                          |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customer          | Account relationships, financial inquiries, contacts, setup and attachments.             | Contact information, Open transactions, Balance and Attachments enabled.                                                  |
| Sell              | Sales quotations, orders and related sales information.                                  | Sales orders enabled, filtered to the selected customer; existing form reused.                                            |
| Invoice           | Free text invoicing, posted invoice journals, recurring templates and related inquiries. | Hidden pending confirmed invoice forms. Sales-order invoicing remains in its existing order workflow.                     |
| Collect           | Customer payments, statements, settlement, plus collection/interest/deduction modules.   | Hidden: no payment-journal/settlement frontend exists. The read-only open inquiry is available from Customer and Balance. |
| Service           | Technician visits, service orders, service agreements and service objects.               | Excluded from basic ERP. Both supplied Service images are identical.                                                      |
| Market            | Campaigns and customer/prospect marketing activities.                                    | Excluded from basic ERP. No expanded Market image was supplied.                                                           |
| Commerce          | Retail/call-center/e-commerce customer functionality.                                    | Excluded from basic ERP. No expanded Commerce image was supplied.                                                         |
| General           | Shared record-related commands; exact groups depend on the form.                         | No expanded image supplied; no speculative commands added.                                                                |
| Credit management | Credit limits, blocking rules and collections administration.                            | Advanced workflows excluded; the existing basic credit limit is shown in Balance.                                         |
| Options           | Record information and audit/personalization utilities.                                  | Existing shared record information/audit controls reused.                                                                 |
