# Financial Posting Effects

The expense journal uses SAR 402.50 inclusive of tax: SAR 350 expense and SAR 52.50 input tax, followed by payment. The purchase example uses 100 units at SAR 25 plus SAR 50 delivery expense: SAR 382.50 tax and SAR 2932.50 payment. Accounts come from posting configuration. TaxJournalTrans records calculated journal detail and does not create a second tax voucher or additional accounting lines.
