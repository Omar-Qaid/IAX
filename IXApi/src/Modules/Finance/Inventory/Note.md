1. Product Information Management
Core
- Product master and released products.
- Item and service product types, with appropriate transaction restrictions.
- Product number, name, search name, description, and status.
- Product variants: Configuration, Size, Color, and Style, no needed.
- Product categories and item groups.
- Product identifiers and barcodes.
- Product release to legal entities/companies.
2. Inventory Setup and Configuration
Core
- Inventory parameters.
- Units of measure and unit conversions.
- Item model groups and costing methods.
- Storage dimension groups: Site, Warehouse, Location, where applicable.
- Tracking dimension groups: Batch and Serial number, where required.
- Inventory statuses and basic inventory defaults.
- Number sequences and required posting setup.

Inventory Costing and Financial Integration
- Basic costing methods, such as FIFO, weighted average, and standard cost where implemented.
- Inventory cost calculation and adjustment.
- Inventory value reports.
- Inventory posting profiles and ledger integration, where General Ledger is available.
- Reconciliation between inventory transactions, inventory value, and financial postings.
